# Topic 14 — Mathematics & Logical Reasoning

> 🏢 **Why it matters:** Almost every BD trainee exam (Cefalo, TigerIT, DataSoft, BJIT especially) has an **aptitude section** — 8–15 questions on arithmetic, percentages, ratios, series, probability, and logic puzzles. These are fast marks with the right shortcuts and no coding needed.

## Percentages, ratios, averages

- **x% of y = (x/100)·y.** 15% of 200 = 30.
- **Percentage change = (new − old)/old × 100.**
- **Increase then decrease by same %:** net is a **loss** (e.g., +10% then −10% → −1%).
- **Average = sum / count.** If avg of n numbers is A, sum = n·A.
- **Ratio a:b split of total T:** parts = T·a/(a+b) and T·b/(a+b).

🧠 **Successive % A then B → net = A + B + (A·B)/100** (signs matter).

## Speed, time, work

- **Distance = Speed × Time.** Average speed (equal distances) = **2ab/(a+b)** (harmonic mean).
- **Work:** if A does a job in `a` days, A's rate = 1/a per day. A+B together = 1/a + 1/b per day → time = **ab/(a+b)**.

## Number properties

- **Prime:** divisible only by 1 and itself (2 is the only even prime).
- **LCM × HCF = product of the two numbers.**
- **Divisibility:** by 3 (digit sum ÷3), by 9 (digit sum ÷9), by 4 (last two digits), by 5 (ends 0/5).
- **Even/odd:** even±even=even, odd±odd=even, even±odd=odd. odd×odd=odd.

## Permutations & combinations (basics)

- **Permutation** (order matters): nPr = n!/(n−r)!.
- **Combination** (order doesn't): nCr = n!/(r!(n−r)!).
- **Factorial:** 5! = 120. Arrangements of n distinct items = n!.
- 🧠 **"Arrangement/order" → permutation; "selection/group" → combination.**

## Probability (basics)

- **P(event) = favorable / total**, between 0 and 1.
- **P(A or B)** (mutually exclusive) = P(A) + P(B). **P(A and B)** (independent) = P(A)·P(B).
- **P(not A) = 1 − P(A).**
- Coin: P(head) = 1/2. Die: P(specific number) = 1/6. Two dice sum: 36 outcomes.

## Series / sequences (pattern spotting)

```
2, 4, 8, 16, __   → ×2 → 32 (geometric)
1, 4, 9, 16, __   → squares → 25
2, 3, 5, 7, 11,__ → primes → 13
1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, __ → Fibonacci → 13
1, 8, 27, 64, __  → cubes → 125
```
🧠 Check for: +constant (arithmetic), ×constant (geometric), squares/cubes, primes, Fibonacci, alternating patterns.

## Logical reasoning types

- **Syllogisms:** "All A are B; all B are C → all A are C." Watch for "some/no".
- **Blood relations:** draw a family tree; "father's sister = aunt."
- **Coding-decoding:** letter shifts (CAT→DBU is +1 each).
- **Odd one out**, **analogies** (A is to B as C is to ?).
- **Direction sense:** track turns (right of North = East).

## Common mistakes & tricks

- ❌ "+10% then −10% returns to start" → **net −1%**.
- ❌ Confusing permutation (order) with combination (no order).
- ❌ Average speed = (a+b)/2 → for equal distances it's **2ab/(a+b)**.
- 🧠 **nCr: order doesn't matter (committee). nPr: order matters (ranking).**
- 🧠 **LCM × HCF = product of numbers.**

## 📄 Cheat sheet
```
x% of y = xy/100 | %change=(new-old)/old·100 | successive: A+B+AB/100
Avg=sum/count | Ratio a:b of T → Ta/(a+b) | Dist=Speed×Time
Work together: ab/(a+b) days | Avg speed(equal dist)=2ab/(a+b)
nPr=n!/(n-r)! (order) | nCr=n!/(r!(n-r)!) (no order) | 5!=120
P=fav/total, 0..1 | P(notA)=1-P(A) | independent: P(A)·P(B)
Series: ×2 geometric, squares, cubes, primes, Fibonacci
LCM×HCF=product | 2=only even prime | div by 3 = digitsum÷3
```
